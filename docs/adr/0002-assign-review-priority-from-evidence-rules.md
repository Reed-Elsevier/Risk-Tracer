# Assign review priority from evidence rules

RiskTracer assigns its initial review priority from explicit signal categories: High for a direct entity watchlist match or at least two independent categories, Medium for one category, and No flagged signals for none. The categories are possible duplicate submission, indirect watchlist exposure, and PO integrity; exception history is shown as context and must not double-count an already detected duplicate. A reviewer may override the priority with a reason, while the LLM explains cited evidence and never sets the initial priority. This keeps the prototype's triage reproducible and reviewable while retaining natural-language explanations and human judgment.
